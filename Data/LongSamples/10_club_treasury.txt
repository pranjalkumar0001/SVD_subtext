Student Society Treasury Handover Note

This note describes how the outgoing treasurer will transfer the student society accounts to the next committee. The purpose is to make the financial records understandable without relying on private messages or the memory of a single person. The handover should explain the opening balance, money received, payments made, outstanding commitments, and the balance available for future activities. It should distinguish money already spent from money reserved for an approved event.

Every payment should be supported by a receipt or another appropriate record. The expense register should include the date, amount, purpose, and person who approved it. A reimbursement request is not the same as evidence that the payment has been completed. Where a receipt is missing, the entry should describe the problem and the action required. The next committee should not have to guess whether an unexplained withdrawal was an event expense, a refund, or an error.

The treasurer will compare the expense register with the bank statement and investigate differences. Some differences may arise because a payment has been authorised but has not yet appeared in the account. Others may indicate duplicate entries or incorrect amounts. The reconciliation should identify each unresolved item individually. A matching final balance is useful, but it does not prove that every transaction has been classified correctly. Supporting documents must remain connected to their corresponding entries.

Planning the next event requires a separate budget. Expected registration income should be recorded as an estimate until payments arrive. Venue charges, equipment rental, refreshments, printing, and travel may need different approval arrangements. The budget should include a reasonable allowance for uncertain costs without presenting that allowance as an expense already incurred. If income is lower than expected, the committee should identify which optional activities can be reduced before committing to additional purchases.

Access to the bank account and accounting records must be transferred through the authorised process. Passwords should not be included in an ordinary handover document or sent to the entire society mailing list. The committee should confirm which members can view records, approve spending, and make payments. Departing members should lose access when their responsibilities end. Copies of receipts and statements should be stored in an agreed location with an appropriate backup.

Before the handover is closed, the incoming treasurer should reproduce the reconciliation and ask about every outstanding item. The committee will sign a record of the documents received and the questions still unresolved. This process is intended to preserve continuity and accountability. The supporting records should remain available to authorised committee members after the handover, so that later questions can be answered without reconstructing the entire accounting history.
